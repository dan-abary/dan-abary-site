A Los Angeles-based designer whose work spans image making, drawing, graphic design, art direction, and typography. Dan began his creative practice as an artist and musician in NYC, and in recent years has worked as a brand designer, leading identity and campaign work across health, tech, fashion, entertainment, and cultural institutions. Fluent in strategy and concept, he looks for solutions where culture, commerce, and communication meet.
